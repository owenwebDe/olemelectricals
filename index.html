What we do

Three scales, one standard

A bungalow and a factory are the same job at different sizes: load worked out, cable sized for it, protection that matches, and a test before we hand over.

01

Domestic wiring

New builds and rewires — circuits, boards, sockets and lighting, with the old wiring taken out properly rather than buried.

02

Commercial installations

Offices, shops and plazas. Risers, cable tray, lighting and power to every desk — sequenced so the floor can keep trading.

03

Industrial wiring

Factories and workshops: three-phase distribution, motor circuits, control panels and the protection that keeps them alive.

04

Consultancy

Load calculations, drawings and a second opinion — before you commit to a design, a contractor or a generator you may not need.

Materials

Buy the materials from us too

We supply what we fit. Buy a reel of cable or a full bill of materials for a building — priced against what the job actually needs, so you are not left with four boxes of the wrong breaker.

  • 01

    Cable and conduit

    Single core, flex and armoured, by the metre or the full reel, with the conduit, trunking and tray to run it in.

  • 02

    Breakers and MCBs

    MCBs, RCDs and the main switchgear, rated for the circuit rather than for the shelf.

  • 03

    Distribution boards

    Boards and enclosures from two ways to a full industrial panel.

  • 04

    Lighting and fittings

    Fittings, drivers and lamps for a room, a floor or a yard.

  • 05

    Switches and sockets

    Accessories, back boxes and the plates to finish them.

  • 06

    Earthing and protection

    Rods, clamps, bonding and surge protection — the part of the installation nobody asks for until something is damaged.

How it works

Quote, deposit, test, handover

You know the price before anything starts, and you pay the balance after the installation has been tested — not before.

01

Send the job

WhatsApp us photographs of the board, the room or the site — or the drawings if you have them. Use the form below and it arrives as one message.

02

Site visit and written quote

We come and measure the load. You get an itemised quote — labour, materials and a timeline — in writing, before you commit.

03

Deposit, then we book your dates

Agree the quote and pay the deposit by transfer or card. That is what reserves the crew; materials are ordered against your list the same week.

04

Test, hand over, pay the balance

Every circuit is tested and the board is labelled before we leave. The balance falls due on handover, and you get a record of what was installed.

Why people call us

Almost every job comes by referral

Olem Electrical has not needed to advertise. The work arrives because the last client told someone — a landlord, a site manager, a neighbour. That is a slow way to grow and a hard standard to keep, and it is the reason the testing at the end is not optional.
